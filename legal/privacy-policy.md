# Privacy Policy — MagicBox

**DRAFT — please have a lawyer review before publishing.**

_Last updated: 14 July 2026_

Nhancio Technologies Private Limited ("we", "us"), Hyderabad, Telangana, India, operates MagicBox (the "Service"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

## 1. Data we collect
- **Account data:** name, email, profile photo (via Google Sign-In).
- **Content you provide:** brand details, briefs, uploaded photos, generated posts/images/videos.
- **Connected-account data:** tokens and profile info for social accounts you connect (Instagram, LinkedIn). Access tokens are stored encrypted and are never exposed to the browser.
- **Billing data:** handled by Dodo Payments (merchant of record). We do **not** store card numbers.
- **Usage & device data:** pages, clicks, and events via product analytics (PostHog), plus standard log data.

## 2. How we use it
To provide and improve the Service, generate and publish content you request, process subscriptions, provide support, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

## 3. Processors / third parties we share with
- **Google / Firebase** — authentication, database, hosting, storage.
- **Google Vertex AI (Gemini, Imagen, Veo)** — AI generation of text/images/video from your inputs.
- **Dodo Payments** — subscription billing (merchant of record).
- **Brevo** — transactional email.
- **PostHog** — product analytics.
- **Meta (Instagram) & LinkedIn** — only when you connect them, to publish content you approve.

## 4. Retention
We keep data while your account is active and for as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. You can request deletion (see Rights).

## 5. Your rights
Depending on your location (e.g. GDPR/CCPA) you may access, correct, export, or delete your data, and object to or restrict processing. Contact hello@nhancio.com. You can disconnect social accounts and delete generated content at any time in the app.

## 6. Cookies & analytics
We use cookies/local storage for authentication and analytics. Where required, we request consent before non-essential tracking.

## 7. Security
Access tokens and API keys are stored server-side only; social tokens are not client-readable. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. International transfers
We are based in India. Data may be processed in India and in other countries where our processors (listed above, e.g. Google, Dodo Payments, PostHog) operate. Where required, appropriate safeguards are applied for cross-border transfers.

## 9. Children
The Service is not directed to anyone under 18.
