## Free-to-Paid Conversion Strategy

1. **Usage Limits**: Free users hit generation limits, prompting upgrade
2. **Watermark Removal**: Free videos have "Made with MagicBox AI" watermark
3. **Resolution Gating**: 720p free, 1080p+ paid
4. **Template Access**: Best-performing templates reserved for paid users
5. **Feature Teasing**: Show AI-optimized hooks/captions as "Pro" features
6. **Social Proof**: Show conversion metrics "Pro users get 3x more engagement"
