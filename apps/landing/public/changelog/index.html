MagicBox 1.0 — Autonomous Marketing & UGC Distribution

We are proud to release MagicBox 1.0! Built for founders, creators, and growth teams, MagicBox automates the entire social marketing pipeline — from website brand analysis and multi-format content creation to approval and multi-platform publishing.

🧠 Maya 1.0 Autopilot

Learns your brand from your website URL and crafts tailor-made post suggestions ready for review.

👉 Swipe Approval

Tinder-style intuitive interface to approve, edit, or regenerate content in seconds.

🌐 4+ Social Channels

Direct publishing to Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Pages.

🎨 Creative Studio

Carousels, AI visuals with Imagen 3, video ads with Veo, and UGC copy rewrites.

✨ Highlights in Version 1.0

  • Maya Autonomous Marketing Agent: Scrapes landing pages to deduce target audience, core hooks, brand personality, and visual style. Generates continuous content suggestions automatically.
  • Direct Social Publishing Pipeline: Direct verified OAuth connections with Instagram (Feed & Reels), LinkedIn (Posts & Articles), YouTube (Shorts & Videos), and Facebook Pages.
  • Creative Studio & Multi-Slide Carousels: Create high-engagement swipeable carousels with custom layers, brand badges, and multi-ratio exports (1:1, 4:5, 9:16).
  • AI Video & UGC Creation: Google Veo generative video clips combined with Remotion dynamic video compositing for short-form product reels and ads.
  • Centralized Brand Kit: Upload brand logos, custom colors, tone of voice presets, and company bios to ensure consistent outputs.
  • Content Calendar & Automations: Schedule recurring automations with visual drag-and-drop calendar queue management.
  • Real-time Reactive Engine (Convex): Instant sync across devices with zero refresh lag, robust retry policies, and tenant-scoped data isolation.
  • Unified Credits System: Transparent i-credits (images & copy) and v-credits (video duration) with an instant 7-day free trial on signup.

🛡️ Performance & Security

  • Sub-second Page Speeds: Static server-side prerendered architecture with optimized bundles.
  • Enterprise-grade Security: Server-side token encryption, strict CSP headers, App Check, and CSRF protection.
