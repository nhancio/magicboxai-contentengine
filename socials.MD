# Social Media Strategy — MagicBoxAI

## 1. Product Name & Category
**Product:** MagicBoxAI
**Category:** AI UGC Video Creation Platform

## 2. Target Audience
**Who are we selling to?**
- Content creators and influencers looking to scale production.
- Small business owners needing high-quality ads without high costs.
- Performance marketers testing multiple UGC-style hooks.

## 3. Primary Distribution Channels
- **TikTok & Instagram Reels:** Showcase the actual AI-generated video outputs.
- **LinkedIn:** B2B marketing use cases and ROI discussions for agencies.
- **Product Hunt:** For the initial "build in public" launch and tech community engagement.
- **Twitter/X:** Engaging with the AI community and sharing "Prompt-to-Video" demos.

## 4. Content Strategy
- **60-Second Ad Demos:** "Watch me create a UGC ad for this product in under a minute."
- **AI Avatar Showcase:** Highlighting the realism and variety of available avatars.
- **Script-to-Video Transformation:** Showing the process from a simple text prompt to a finished video.
- **Problem/Solution Demos:** "Stop paying $500 for a single UGC video—do it for $10 with MagicBox."

## 5. Growth Hack / Viral Loop
**The "Share for Credits" Loop:**
Provide users with free generation credits for every video they share on social media that includes the #MagicBoxAI hashtag. This creates a stream of organic UGC demonstrating the product's capabilities.
